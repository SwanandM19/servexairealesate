The challenge

Where bookings quietly slip away

You already pay to generate enquiries through ads, portals, hoardings and channel partners. The money is usually lost later, in the gap between an enquiry arriving and someone following it through.

Enquiries arrive everywhere

Website forms, portal leads, ad lead forms, WhatsApp, calls, walk-ins, channel partners. Each one lives in a different inbox, sheet or phone, and nobody sees them all together.

Slow first replies

Buyers enquire with several projects at once. If your reply comes hours later, or on Monday for a Saturday-night enquiry, the site visit often goes to whoever answered first.

Follow-ups live in memory

After the first call, the next step depends on an agent remembering. Warm prospects go cold, not because they lost interest but because nobody called back.

Leads leave with people

When a sales executive leaves, their WhatsApp chats, call notes and active prospects often leave with them. You paid for those leads.

Managers see numbers late

"How many site visits this week? Who's following up on what?" Someone spends hours putting it together in Excel, and by then the week is over.

Marketing spend you can't trace

Which campaign, portal or partner actually produced site visits and bookings, and which only produced phone numbers? Without tracking, the budget is spread on guesswork.

Straight answers

Questions directors usually ask

We already use a CRM. Do we need to replace it?

Not necessarily. If your CRM works but isn't connected to WhatsApp, your website or your reporting, we can look at extending and integrating it. If it's the reason things are slipping, we'll say so plainly and explain the options.

Will our sales team actually use it?

Adoption is the main reason we build around your existing process instead of forcing a new one. Fewer fields, mobile-friendly screens and WhatsApp built into the workflow mean agents do less admin, not more. We also train the team at rollout.

Can it capture leads from portals and ad campaigns?

Website forms and ad lead forms can usually be connected directly. For property portals, it depends on what export or integration each portal offers you. We check this for your specific sources during scoping and tell you exactly what can be automated.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on scope. We recommend starting with the core enquiry pipeline so your team gets value early, then adding chatbot, automation and reporting in phases. You'll get a timeline with the scoped proposal.

What does it cost?

The consultation is free. Cost depends on what you need, whether that's a single pipeline with reminders or a CRM with chatbot, integrations and dashboards. After mapping your process, you get a clear written scope and quote before you commit to anything.

Who can see our lead data?

Role-based access is part of the design, so agents see their leads, managers see their teams and directors see everything. Hosting, access and data handling are agreed with you during scoping.
